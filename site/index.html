An honest note: this is a proof of concept

OligoCrystal is in early research and development. Nothing is for sale yet, and we are not accepting DNA samples.

We have made first test crystals in glass from common salts, with and without DNA. We have not yet shown:

  • how much of the added DNA stays in the finished insert, or for how long;
  • what the crystals are made of, or whether DNA changes them;
  • how well a finished piece holds up to everyday wear, light, heat and humidity.

We will only take orders after we have tested these things, and we will publish what we find, including the results that don't go our way.

How it is planned to work

This is the process we are developing. Details may change as testing goes on.

  1. 1. Mail-in sample

    You would mail a sample straight to us. We are still choosing the simplest at-home collection method.

  2. 2. Purification

    In our own workspace we purify DNA from your sample. We don't sequence it or run genetic tests on it.

  3. 3. Crystal insert

    The purified DNA is mixed into a crystal-forming solution of magnesium or ammonium sulfate and slowly dried into a crystalline layer.

  4. 4. Glass pendant

    The insert is sealed inside a double-walled glass necklace or pair of earrings, designed to catch the light.

Questions

Can I order now?

No. We are not taking orders or samples yet. Join the waitlist and we will email you when that changes.

Is my DNA really in the finished piece?

That is the goal, and it is the first thing we are testing. Right now we cannot say how much DNA stays in the crystal insert, or for how long. We won't claim it does until we have checked it ourselves.

Aren't the crystals just salt?

Mostly, yes. The color you see comes from salt crystals such as magnesium sulfate (Epsom salt) or ammonium sulfate. The DNA is added to the crystal-forming solution; it is not what makes the color. We are testing whether DNA changes how the crystals look.

What happens to my DNA sample?

Nothing yet, because we are not accepting samples. In the process we are planning, your sample would be used for one thing only: making your piece. We would not sequence it, test it for health or ancestry, sell it or share it. Before we accept a single sample we will publish a full written policy on how samples are labeled, stored, used and destroyed, and how long that takes.

What about my privacy?

This website has no sign-up form, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. If you email us to join the waitlist, we keep your email address only to tell you about OligoCrystal, and we will delete it if you ask. See the privacy notice.

Can someone learn things about me from the pendant?

We don't read or record any genetic information. Whether DNA could be recovered from a finished piece is one of the questions our testing will cover. Until then, we make no promises either way.

How durable will it be?

We don't know yet. We will test sealed pieces against wear, light, heat and humidity before we sell any, and we will tell you how to care for them.

Who is behind this?

OligoCrystal is a research project by a scientist at YMG Consulting Services. It is separate from YMG's analytical chemistry consulting.
